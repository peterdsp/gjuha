Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 October 2026. Applies to Gjuha for iPhone and iPad, bundle identifier com.gjuha.app, and its widget extension.

Gjuha collects nothing. It has no servers, no accounts and no analytics, and it makes no network requests at all. Everything you do in the app stays on your device, and you can delete all of it in one tap.

What this means in practice

Gjuha is an offline app. The entire course is in the app download. There is no backend to send anything to, no sign-in, no advertising, no third-party analytics or attribution SDK, and no tracking of any kind. No App Tracking Transparency prompt is shown, because there is nothing to track.

Information stored on your device

The app saves the following locally, so your progress survives closing and reopening it. None of it leaves your device.

WhatWhy
Lessons completed, XP, day streak, XP earned todayTo show your progress and your daily goal.
Your spaced repetition scheduleTo bring each word back for review at the right time.
Achievements you have earned, and whenSo a milestone you reached is not taken back later.
Your chosen daily goal and the reason you gave during onboardingTo set your goal and tailor the course.
Your daily reminder time, if you turn reminders onTo schedule the reminder. Off by default.
An in-progress lessonSo you return to the same question if the app is closed mid-lesson.

The Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets

If you add a widget, the app writes a small summary (your streak, total XP, how many words are due, words mastered, today's XP and goal, and the title of your next lesson) into a shared container on your device so the widget can draw it. That summary stays on your device and is rebuilt from your own progress. Removing the app removes it.

Notifications

The daily study reminder is off unless you turn it on. When you do, the reminder is scheduled by iOS on your device at the time you choose. It contains no personal information, and nothing about it is sent anywhere. You can turn it off at any time in Profile, or in the Settings app.

Grammar explanations generated on your device

On devices that support it, Gjuha can generate a tailored grammar explanation using Apple's on-device language model. The text is produced on your device and is never uploaded. When the model is unavailable, the app falls back to an explanation built from the lesson content it already ships. Either way nothing is sent off the device, and explanations never affect how an answer is graded.

Permissions Gjuha does not ask for

Gjuha requests no access to your microphone, camera, photos, contacts, calendars, location, health data or motion data. It declares no usage descriptions for any of them. The only permission it ever asks for is notifications, and only if you turn the daily reminder on.

Children

Gjuha is suitable for all ages and is rated 4+. It has no user-generated content, no social features, no chat, no web browsing, no advertising and no purchases. Because it collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.

Third parties

Gjuha uses one open-source software library, The Composable Architecture, which is compiled into the app and is an application architecture tool. It performs no networking and collects no data. There are no advertising networks, analytics providers, crash reporters or attribution services in the app.

Your control over your data

Because everything is on your device, you control all of it:

  • Open Profile and tap Reset learning data to erase your XP, streak, completed lessons, review schedule and achievements. The action asks for confirmation and cannot be undone.
  • Deleting the app removes everything it stored, including the widget summary.

There is no account to close and no request to send us, because we hold nothing about you.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Gjuha ever adds a network feature, an account system or anything that collects data, this policy and the app's App Store privacy information will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top of this page will change.
