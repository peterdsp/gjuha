gjuha is Albanian for the language. Albanian is spoken by around eight million people and has no major learning app. Gjuha is being built to change that: an offline-first course for iPhone and iPad, for adults who want to actually remember what they study.

Built to stick

Every word you learn enters a spaced repetition schedule and comes back at widening intervals, prioritising the words you keep getting wrong. Your study time goes where it is needed rather than where it feels comfortable.

Progress you can trust

Most apps treat "seen once" and "learned" as the same thing. Gjuha keeps three separate figures: words seen, words in review, and words mastered. Mastered means a word has survived to a documented long-term interval, and nothing else is presented as mastery.

Grammar explained, not just drilled

Albanian has postposed definite articles, five cases and verb moods most Western learners have never met. Gjuha explains the why, with conjugation tables and real examples, and keeps explanation strictly separate from grading.

Works entirely offline

No account, no servers, no analytics, no ads, no subscriptions. The whole course is in the app, so it works on a plane and in Shkodër with no signal. Everything lives on your device and you can delete all of it in one tap.

Status

Gjuha is in preparation for its first release, and this page will say plainly what is in it rather than what is planned.

The first release is the A1 foundation: a vocabulary of around 350 reviewed entries, the grammar guides that go with them, and the full spaced repetition engine. The course is structured as twelve units reaching B2, and the later units fill in through updates. Pronunciation audio arrives once a native speaker has reviewed every clip, which is why the app would rather stay silent on a word than play a synthetic voice that mispronounces Albanian.
