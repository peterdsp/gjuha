### UI tests

`xcodebuild test ... -only-testing:GjuhaUITests`

- Before: the target contained a `setUpWithError` and nothing else. Zero tests.
- After: **6 tests, all passing, TEST SUCCEEDED** (onboarding to lesson map;
  onboarding not repeating after completion; every root tab opening; Profile
  separating exposure from mastery; opening a lesson and leaving early; the reset
  confirmation being cancellable).
- Three non-obvious things had to be handled to make these deterministic, and all
  three were found by the tests failing honestly rather than being guessed at:
  1. **A view that animates forever never reaches quiescence**, so XCUITest's
     synthesised taps were being swallowed and onboarding looked like it was
     ignoring button presses. Five perpetual decorative animations ignored Reduce
     Motion; fixing that (a real accessibility defect, see below) was also what
     fixed the tests.
  2. **The launch splash renders the real UI beneath it at `opacity(0.001)`**, so
     controls report `exists == true` while still covered. The `tap` helper waits
     for `isHittable`, not existence.
  3. **A SwiftUI `.alert` title is the alert element's own label**, not a
     top-level `staticText`, and the Profile reset control sits below the fold.
     The helper scrolls when an element exists but is not hittable.
- One test assertion of mine was simply wrong and the app was right: it demanded a
  confirmation when leaving a lesson with nothing answered. `LessonFeature` only
  confirms once `currentIndex > 0 || xpEarned > 0`, because the dialog exists to
  protect answered questions, not to make leaving difficult. The test now asserts
  that documented behaviour instead.

### Core learning loop

Walked through on iPhone 15 (iOS 27.0 simulator). All steps passed.

- Onboarding: three pages (welcome, "why are you learning Albanian" goal choice,
  daily goal choice). Selecting a goal and finishing lands on Home with the lesson
  path and the streak/XP header. Page indicator advances correctly.
- Home (fresh): no Daily Review card (nothing due yet). First lesson node unlocked,
  later nodes locked.
- Lesson: all three answer outcomes render with distinct icons, not color only.
  Correct shows a green banner with the earned XP (combo multiplier appears on
  streaks). Wrong shows a red banner, reveals the answer, costs a heart. A typed
  near miss with a dropped diacritic (for example "naten e mire") shows the amber
  near miss banner, calls out ë/ç, and is not accepted; typing the correct
  "natën e mirë" is then accepted. Exercise types seen: matching, multiple choice
  (both directions), type the translation, word order.
- Completion: "Lesson Complete" summary with total XP and best combo. Back on Home
  the lesson shows complete, the next lesson unlocks, streak and XP update.
- Profile: six stat cards present: Day Streak 1, Total XP 92, Words seen 10,
  Lessons Done 1, In review 10, Mastered 0. The ten words from the completed lesson

- Relaunch: terminate and relaunch goes straight to Home (onboarding not repeated);
  all progress and the six Profile stats persist.

Observations (not defects introduced by this work):
- A near miss costs a heart like a wrong answer. This is the intended Phase 0 rule
  (a dropped ë or ç changes the word, so it earns no credit); the feedback is still
  distinct. Flagged as a product choice, not a bug.
- On a type the translation exercise the Check button's resting frame sits in the
  keyboard band, so on-device keyboard dismissal there is worth a hardware check.
  Pre-existing layout, not changed by this work.

### Spaced repetition review loop

Reviews become due the day after a word is learned, so the review card and session
are exercised at runtime by seeding a schedule with due items (three due words plus
one already mastered and not due) into the app's on disk defaults, then launching.
Walked through on iPhone 15. All steps passed.

- Home shows a "Daily review" card ("3 words ready to strengthen") only when words
  are due. The seeded mastered word that is not due is correctly excluded from the
  count.
- Tapping the card opens a review that reuses the normal lesson exercise UI, with
  one question per due word, mixing types (multiple choice, type in, fill in blank).
- Review specific behavior confirmed, distinct from a course lesson: a wrong answer
  does not cost a heart, does not end the session, and the session advances to the
  next word rather than forcing a retry. There is no out of hearts failure screen.
- Completion shows the usual summary with the XP earned. Back on Home, the card
  updates: answering one of three wrong left that word due today, so the card
  correctly showed "1 word" afterward; answering the last due word correctly made
  the card disappear (0 due).
- Profile reflected the honest ladder: after the session, In review 4 (the three
  seeded due words plus the mastered one), Mastered 1 (the seeded mastered word),
  Day Streak 1 (a review counts as activity but the day was already active).

Defect found and fixed during verification:
- The review completion screen showed earned XP but that XP was not added to the
  lifetime Total XP (it stayed at 92). Fixed by banking review XP through a new
  `ProgressStore.addXP` path (`recordReviewActivity`), covered by a unit test and
  re-verified at runtime (Total XP increases by the review's XP after a review).
