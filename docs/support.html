Support

Something not working, or a question about the course? Write to info@peterdsp.dev and you will get a reply.

Reporting a problem

So it can be fixed quickly, please include:

  • Your device and iOS version (Settings › General › About)
  • The Gjuha version, shown at the bottom of Profile
  • What you did, what happened, and what you expected instead
  • A screenshot, if it is something you can see

Common questions

Does Gjuha work offline?

Yes, entirely. The whole course is in the app download. There is no sign-in and no network connection is ever used, so it works on a plane and in places with no signal.

How do I change my daily goal?

Open Profile and pick a different goal. The ring on the Learn screen updates to measure against the new one immediately. Reviews count toward your goal as well as lessons, so a day spent strengthening words you already met still counts.

What is the difference between words seen, in review, and mastered?

They are three different things, kept separate on purpose:

  • Words seen is how many distinct words the lessons you finished have covered. Exposure, not memory.
  • In review is how many of those have entered the spaced repetition schedule.
  • Mastered is the subset that has survived to the schedule's long-term interval. Nothing else is described as mastery.

Why did I lose my streak?

A streak counts consecutive days with study activity, in your device's local time. Finishing a lesson or a review session both count. If a whole calendar day passes with neither, the streak restarts at one. Achievements you already earned are kept: a month you actually studied stays recorded even if the current streak resets.

I answered correctly but it was marked as "almost".

Albanian diacritics matter, so ë and ç are required, as are definite articles. A near miss means your answer was recognised but not accepted, and the feedback points at exactly what differed. Capitalisation, extra spaces and accents typed in a different Unicode form are all accepted.

Why is there no audio on some words?

Pronunciation audio ships only for recordings that a native speaker has reviewed, and that review is still in progress. Rather than play a synthetic voice that mispronounces Albanian, the app leaves those words silent and the listening exercises adapt. Audio will arrive in an update.

Why is the Albanian interface marked as needing review?

The app's buttons and labels are translated into Albanian, but those translations are still being checked by a native speaker before being finalised. The teaching content itself is reviewed. If you spot something that reads wrong, telling us is genuinely useful.

How do I delete everything?

Open Profile, scroll to Your Data and tap Reset learning data. It asks for confirmation and clears your XP, streak, completed lessons, review schedule and achievements. Deleting the app removes everything too. See the privacy policy for what is stored and why.

Does Gjuha collect any data about me?

No. There are no servers, no accounts and no analytics, and the app makes no network requests. Details are in the privacy policy.

Does it work on iPad?

Yes. Gjuha is a universal app, and on an iPad the Learn and Profile screens use the extra width rather than stretching a phone layout.

Accessibility

Gjuha supports Dynamic Type at every size, VoiceOver, Reduce Motion, Reduce Transparency and Increase Contrast. Correct, near-miss and incorrect answers are signalled with icons and words as well as colour. If something is still hard to use, please say so: that is a bug worth fixing.
